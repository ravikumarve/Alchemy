The Cognitive Pipeline

⛏️

The Archaeologist

ONLINE

Role: Data Miner | Tools: SemanticChunker, TableExtractor

Executes a rigorous 7-step validation workflow. It extracts raw text using unstructured.io, filters out outdated noise, and structures evergreen content into pristine data chunks.

🏄

The Trend-Jacker

PENDING

Role: Contextualizer | Tools: HookLibrary, AttentionMetrics

Takes historical truth and maps it to modern attention frameworks. Generates viral hooks and restructures the narrative specifically for high-retention social algorithms.

👁️

The Visionary

PENDING

Role: Media Architect | Tools: PromptGenerator, StoryBoarder

Analyzses the context to generate B-roll prompts, visual storyboards, and finalizes the asset packaging for immediate distribution.
