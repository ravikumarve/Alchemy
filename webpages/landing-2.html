The Alchemy Protocol.

ALCHEMY is a production-ready content transmutation system that extracts evergreen data from legacy documents while intelligently filtering outdated context .

  • ✦ 60-Second Engine: Perfectly timed content processing for short-form video .
  • ✦ Zero-Maintenance: Runs as a cron job or background daemon for unattended operation .
  • ✦ CPU-Optimized: Engineered to run perfectly on hardware like a Dell Latitude 3460 without requiring a GPU .
